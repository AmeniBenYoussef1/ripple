Ripple sample pack: HerCode Hackathon Paris 2026 (fictional)
All people, emails (@example.com), companies and addresses are invented.

1. On Ripple's Start page, upload these 5 files:
   participants.csv, people.csv, schedule.json, catering_contract.pdf, budget.json
2. In "What went wrong?", paste the content of problem1_venue_email.txt
   (the venue cancels because of a water leak), or write your own problem, e.g.
   "Lina's flight from Tunis is delayed by 3 hours." or "25 more people just registered."
